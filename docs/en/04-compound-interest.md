# Compound interest

Compound interest is interest calculated on both the original amount of money and the interest that has already accumulated. Unlike simple interest, which only applies to the principal, compound interest causes savings or debt to grow at an accelerating rate over time. The more frequently interest compounds, such as daily versus annually, the faster the balance grows.
